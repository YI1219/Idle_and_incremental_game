[Current rework evidence](../art/review/terrain_fidelity/2026-10-06/rework/review.md) records fresh captures, checks and costs; the [previous report](../art/review/terrain_fidelity/2026-10-06/review.md) remains historical evidence.

The workshop uses `TerrainData`, a sparse 2D XZ dictionary of cells with height and optional water elevation. It is not a 3D voxel volume. Missing cells mean holes or off-island space. All visible land, automatic detail, supported props and picking share this authoritative data. The retained legacy gameplay scene has separate historical terrain rules.

Raise/lower move existing cells by 0.5 units per stroke, bounded from -4 to 12. Expand fills absent cells at the selected height; contract removes cells, water and automatic detail. Drag strokes interpolate their path and change each visited cell once. Undo/redo retain 64 strokes. Optional snapping aligns brush centers to picked cells, including cliff-boundary ownership. With snapping off (the default), circular coverage follows the cursor continuously; heights remain in 0.5-unit steps. The optional construction grid does not define the surface material.

Product camera orientation is fixed. Keyboard and middle drag pan horizontally without changing height. Only the wheel changes height/zoom; recenter/reset/presets preserve height. The look-development harness manually rotates the camera for inspection without enabling gameplay orbit. See [camera behavior](camera.md).

Water painting offers exactly eight absolute elevations: 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3 and 3.5. Only lower floors accept water; raising the floor through its water surface removes water. There are 47 normalized eight-neighbor data states with diagonal normalization. Whole equal-elevation regions render with shared smoothed contours, not individual cell polygons. This is editable pond geometry and shading, not fluid simulation.

A bounded terrain-derived texture carries actual smoothed-contour bank distance in R, floor depth in G, receiving-impact strength in B and wet coverage in A. Lower wet cells beside higher wet cells seed disturbance; a short traversal fades it only through connected receiving water at the same elevation. The texture publishes with the completed immutable terrain snapshot, synchronizing shading with edits. Channels retain their meaning through bank-field refinement.

The lake is opaque and nonmetallic. Controlled normal/color isolation confirmed that the former regular bands came from directional cosine wave slopes through the BRDF. Four continuous 2D height fields with exact derivatives now provide calm and localized receiving-water slopes, converted into view space. Actual bed depth and bank distance shorten the apparent optical path at margins. Roughness remains .16 plus disturbance .06, specular .30. Directional static sky radiance is sky-only; cottage/tree geometry is not reflected. No SSR, probe, emission or refraction layer is added. Uniformly shallow default beds limit depth differentiation.

Outlets facing lower water or absent land generate connected approach aprons, sampled rounded lips and curved falling sheets. Canonical world-height rows preserve XYZ joins across mixed receivers. Run metadata carries across-stream position, world drop, width and actual wet-receiver fraction; clipping preserves it. Fully exposed closed rings skip side fade and retain local landings. An open run whose averaged impact misses receiving water falls back to supported local landings. Sheets have physical folds/lateral curvature and finite downward-moving aeration patches without emission or solid shadows. Outer-contour membership controls shell classification, preventing an edited corner from retaining one obsolete deep column. Wet-mouth clearance limits protrusions behind stream envelopes. All rock volume is part of indexed/pickable terrain; no separate decorative cliff shell is published.

8×8 chunks partition grass, cliff, water, waterfall, shore and foam surfaces. Shared cap triangulation may span remote chunks, so each revision regenerates affected owning chunks. A spatial triangle index bounds chunk work. Complete immutable terrain, decoration, supported props and picking snapshots publish together at two chunks per frame. Continuous strokes finish an in-flight snapshot and coalesce later changes into the next one. Idle frames do not rebuild. Picking intersects actual rendered triangles, resolving owning cells on protruding cliff facets.
