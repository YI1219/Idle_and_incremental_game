The workshop uses `TerrainData`, a sparse 2D XZ dictionary of cells with height and optional water elevation. It is not a 3D voxel volume. Missing cells mean holes or off-island space. All visible land, automatic detail, supported props and picking share this authoritative data. The retained legacy gameplay scene has separate historical terrain rules.

Raise/lower move existing cells by 0.5 units per stroke, bounded from -4 to 12. Expand fills absent cells at the selected height; contract removes cells, water and automatic detail. Drag strokes interpolate their path and change each visited cell once. Undo/redo retain 64 strokes. Optional snapping aligns brush centers to picked cells, including cliff-boundary ownership. With snapping off (the default), circular coverage follows the cursor continuously; heights remain in 0.5-unit steps. The optional construction grid does not define the surface material.
