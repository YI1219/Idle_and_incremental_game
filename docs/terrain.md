# Editable terrain workshop

The active demo uses `TerrainData`: a sparse dictionary of land cells, height and optional water elevation. All visible terrain is generated from this data. Missing cells are off-island or holes. The retained legacy gameplay scene has separate historical terrain rules.

Raise/lower move an existing cell by 0.5 units per stroke; height is bounded from -4 to 12. Expand fills absent cells at the selected height. Contract removes cells, water and automatic decoration. Drag strokes interpolate their path and change each visited cell once. Undo/redo retain the latest 64 strokes.

Snapping aligns the brush center to the picked cell center, including the owning cell on cliff boundaries. With snapping off, circular coverage follows the cursor continuously; underlying terrain still uses cells and 0.5 height increments. The construction grid is optional and does not define ground materials.

Water painting selects one of exactly eight absolute elevations: 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3 or 3.5. Only lower floors accept water; raising the floor through its water surface removes that water. The data retains 47 normalized eight-neighbor states, with diagonal normalization. The active rendering joins whole equal-elevation regions into shared smoothed contours rather than displaying individual cell polygons. Terrain contours support the selected water elevation above neighboring ground, including deep floors. A bounded terrain-derived texture supplies shallow bank and floor-depth color. It publishes with the completed immutable terrain snapshot, so staging cannot update visible water ahead of its mesh. This is editable pond geometry and shading, not fluid simulation. A contour outlet facing lower water or absent land generates an outward spill apron, curved waterfall ribbon and landing foam. Shared world-height flow profiles keep neighboring ribbons joined even when receiving terrain is lowered or removed. Falling water uses moving polygonal veins. Landing foam has joined raised rings with smooth normals and an opacity fade; clipped chunk triangles retain those gradients and normals. The sky is a generated cloud-only backdrop mapped to the fixed camera view, with neutral cubemap lighting. It contains no terrain or props; editable scene objects remain 3D geometry.

`ContourTerrain` builds elevation outlines from authoritative data. World-anchored sampling keeps remote outlines stable during local edits. Hole-aware cap decomposition preserves openings even when a triangulation would surround a small hole. Tapered, irregular cliff vertices form a connected rock volume; inset is limited by thickness of the same filled contour (including holes), so raw missing-cell corners beneath smooth edges do not create untapered blades. Truly narrow necks and isolated cells keep their constrained shell. Width sampling reaches at most 5.5 units in .125 steps, retaining the .28 factor and 1.5 maximum. Per-height winding signs are derived and cleared each refresh to avoid repeated signed-area calculations. Polygon-containment cost still grows with contour complexity. 8×8 chunks partition grass, foam, cliff, water and waterfall surfaces. Cliffs use fixed vertical samples and local profiles, keeping distant rock shapes stable when an edit adds an elevation. Broad contour spans receive lateral subdivisions at no more than .65 units; narrow spans retain their existing profile. A world-anchored clipped-octahedron distance field produces physical rock shoulders and recesses, fading on narrow base profiles to keep opposing walls separated. Geometry and material share the 2.2×1.8×2.2 site dimensions and distance metric. The lower shoulder envelope transitions to a finite slope at the foot and fades base relief to 25%, with reduced residual broad-foot vertical noise. Exposed wet walls limit protrusions behind the stream envelope. Dry mouth-side profiles inspect wet cells within 1.6 units, accepting only cells with a cardinal receiver below their water surface; this catches bends while preserving cliffs beyond intact enclosed-pond banks. Clearance fades at the crest to preserve cap joins. Default reference rays check curtain triangle centers; the contracted-mouth regression tests actual interior segment/triangle crossings in both directions. Legitimate foreground land may occlude an internal spill. Arbitrary layouts and unsampled intersections remain unproven. Shared relief values cache by exact warped sample position and clear each refresh. Broad profiles now reduce horizontal wave noise from .10 to .04, vertical .25 to .09, foot .04 to .02 and profile Y displacement .16 to .06; cellular relief amplitude/offset reduces from 1.02/-.16 to .75/-.10. Narrow-base coefficients remain unchanged. A cellular material supplies stronger color and normal facets across the connected body. Grass caps subtract higher regions so hidden grass cannot protrude through tapered rock. Shared cap triangulation can span remote chunks, so each revision regenerates the active owning chunks. A spatial triangle index limits work within each chunk. Rebuilds are bounded to two chunks per frame; a complete immutable terrain snapshot, its decorations, supported props and picking data publish together. Continuous strokes finish the in-flight snapshot and coalesce newer changes into the next one. Idle frames do not rebuild terrain. Picking intersects the actual rendered triangles, with nearest-cell ownership resolution for exposed facets that protrude beyond a cell boundary.
