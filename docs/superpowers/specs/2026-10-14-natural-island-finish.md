# Natural island finish

User-directed art pass: unify the existing floating-island body with the soft classical Chinese cloud-sea background. Keep original trees/buildings, authoritative island/water data, calm continuous bottom, and sandbox editing. Improve wall/outcrop shape and spacing, grass–stone thickness/contact, water clarity/reflection and falls. No custom original model production, purchases, or new fixed landscape.

Acceptance: representative close rim improves before expanding the finish to the whole island. At least six matched actual-runtime views cover overview, distance, rim, grass–stone contact, lake bank and reverse. Geometry/edit/plant contact/picking remain valid; no new detached geometry or continuous grey lip. Functional acceptance is separate from human art approval and unmeasured target hardware.

Use existing retained models and current procedural terrain. Shade broad stone faces gently while retaining meaningful sharp breaks; reduce triangle-by-triangle paint; prefer unequal grouped rock masses over repeated pillars. Sod should read as compact rooted clumps, with rock exposed locally. Water should have calm depth and view/light-dependent reflection, avoiding outlined noise contours and emitting white curtains.

If custom assets become the decisive missing piece, show the actual limitations and ask in one batch; do not replace the four-view/user-operated Tripo workflow.
