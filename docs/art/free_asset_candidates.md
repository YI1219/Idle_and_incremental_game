# Free asset candidates and current decision

The courtyard image is a look-and-finish reference only. Distill ancient-style presentation from the six games the user listed. See [Scene art and module spec](scene_art_and_module_spec.md). Complete free generic packs remain the first source route; Tripo fills actual custom gaps. Candidate download, trial, production selection, and final acceptance are separate states.

| Candidate | Verified source and contents | Current decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html) | Free Standard downloaded from the official itch page after connectivity was restored. Enclosed License_Standard.txt confirms CC0 1.0 and 68/116 models; actual glTF count is 68. Paid Pro/Source, engine projects, and stylized shaders were not acquired. | Five models tested in three static layouts and three views. Preferred refinement candidate: natural crowns and muted rocks fit better, but noisy foliage, weak ground coverage, placement overlaps, and rendering cost need work. Eight unchanged models are now integrated in the runtime courtyard for further validation. Not final art or target-performance acceptance. |
| [Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) | Original archive downloaded; enclosed license confirms CC0 and version 2.1, while the website changelog lists 1.0. Five unmodified models inspected. | Three-layout trial complete. Sampled block/sphere crowns, orange rocks, and weak grass do not fit well enough for direct adoption. Preserve evidence; do not expand this batch. |
| [Godot Asset Library](https://godotengine.org/asset-library/asset) | Tools, scripts, and resources; licenses vary by entry. | Use only where a relevant helper is needed. Do not treat the engine MIT license as every asset's license. |

The deleted nature four-view batch remains rejected. Do not restore it or require pines, bridges, or water in every courtyard. The existing cottage, character, and sword keep their current source assets.

Next, use trees, rocks, and grass from the same candidate pack to build small scenes that are sparse, dense, and differently adjacent. Unify palette, lighting, scale, and density. Validate actual placement, doorway/traversal rules, camera movement, and normal/dense playable performance. Target Windows thin laptops and MacBook Air remain unmeasured; short development-machine samples do not establish 60 FPS or long-idle suitability.
