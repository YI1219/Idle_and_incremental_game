Reverse wall: from a regimented row to a staggered cliff

Actual Godot output only, native 2560×1440 Forward+, same capture script, cameras, layouts and light on both sides. Before is the 2026-11-21 cliff-water set; after is this package. Target-laptop performance is unmeasured.
