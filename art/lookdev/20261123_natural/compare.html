Known differences and limits

  • The three-island wallpaper view still uses a simpler cloud sea and no far hills: Godot's far field (cloud decks and mountain rings) was authored for its steep review cameras and thins out toward the horizon at the wallpaper's low camera.
  • The far hills at the top left of the whole view sit differently from Godot's.
  • Godot's far depth-of-field blur is not ported; distant things are paler, not blurred.
  • No sparkle glints on the water.
  • The 4,096-cell island with real props costs 10 to 11 ms a frame while moving, close to the 12 ms budget: cut-out leaf cards overdraw, and props have no distance levels yet.
  • Nothing is animated in these stills. Wind, water motion and the falls are built to move and are shown frozen.
  • The island cliffs differ from Godot's on purpose: the body is the new hanging-rock construction from the same cells.
  • Thin shaded joints remain where three terrace steps meet; they show rock, not the inside.
  • All of this is study-tool code, to be rebuilt in the real renderer.
