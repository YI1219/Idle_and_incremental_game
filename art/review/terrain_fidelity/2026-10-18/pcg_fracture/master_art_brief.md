# Existing-source master: shape brief before further PCG

This is a refinement brief, not a new generation task or art acceptance.
Target: `art/source/visual_targets/2026-10-16/cliff_user_target.png`.
Open `art/work/pcg_fracture/2026-10-18/route2/existing_master_refinement.blend`:

An additional rejected refinement is preserved separately at
`art/work/pcg_fracture/2026-10-18/route2_faces/existing_master_refinement.blend`.
It has a stronger major turn but3strict self-crossings and insufficient art
gain. It is evidence, not a qualified source. Existing-source refinement is
permitted; the earlier blanket assignment of every further refinement to the
user is not a project requirement. New custom model creation remains a separate

The current three-pillar shape is rejected. Do not simply extend it downward.

1. Compose unequal main faces, with changes in facing direction and shoulder
   height. The central broad rectangle needs a deliberate major turn, rather
   than a shallow surface groove. Avoid a repeated row of equal upright slabs.
2. Let adjacent faces meet along clefts that change direction and terminate.
   Upper clefts must lead into the lower mass; no uniform full-height channels,
   thin fins, detached pieces or independent rock chips.
3. Shape the complete visible cross-section from grass/soil edge through the
   shoulder to one inward basal return. Keep broad facets readable while the
   overall silhouette flows; remove flat cut feet and lower hanging panels.
   The reference does not show the entire underside, so design that unseen part
   consistently rather than claiming it can be copied exactly.
4. Reserve actual thickness and uneven pockets for the soil/grass join. Do not
   create a continuous green roof projecting over every cliff face. Grass
   geometry comes after this supporting shape is accepted.

Acceptance is actual Godot close/whole/reverse/bottom plus neutral material,
judged against the target. A direct master must be accepted before treating it
as a generator exemplar; then separately measure sampling loss, public edits,
neighbor combinations, at least two contours/two seeds and supported vegetation.
No texture/generation spend is needed before the untextured shape is accepted.

The project's user-operated workflow assigns custom3Dcreation/refinement to
the user and art direction/integration to Codex. This brief supplies a concrete
existing-source starting point. No new custom original model has been selected
or its four-view generation package prepared; do not silently open a new asset
generation task as a substitute for this shape decision.
