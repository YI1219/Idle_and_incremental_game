## Art and full-goal audit

Parent/independent actual-image review rejects art promotion. Physical soil and
supported leaf roots are a useful technical witness, but green/earth strip has
mechanical steps/triangular breaks, source still has broad grey benches and a
rounded heavy hull. Carve creates a step-like notch. Larger uniform scale makes
sampling finer relative to source faces but does not repair their composition.
Source is a narrow ridge with lower stone terraces, rather than a broad calm
upper ground transitioning into unequal cliff faces. More grass/material detail
cannot supply that missing relationship.

This prototype derives cells FROM a fixed master. It is not a compiler FROM
arbitrary authored cell contours. Continuous build/editor integration, picking,
automatic regeneration, two-contour/two-seed art, target-device performance and
user approval of all three art criteria remain incomplete. Only root support,
finite field/history/mesh evidence is added this round. No formal default or
original assets/trees/buildings/layout/water changed; full goal remains active.
