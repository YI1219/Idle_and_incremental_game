## Grass contact

The source grass is narrow when reduced to rim scale: its retained source bounds are approximately.639×1.334×.737m. `grasspatch1` widens its leaves into four low unequal fans rather than the original three narrow fans, with fewer placements. `grasspatch2` combines that with whole-loop arc-length sampling; `grasspatch3` also favors bends. Some front clefts read more clearly, but the reverse develops an almost continuous sharp leaf row, emphasizing the thin cap. Bend selection does not solve this. All three are rejected.

`grasssample1` keeps original source-leaf proportions with whole-loop arc-length sampling. Rejected. `grasssample2` projects fixed jittered world probes onto the boundary, retaining original fans. This passes the two finite retessellation/locality fixtures, but leaves more bare reverse/shore cap and has no sufficient whole/far improvement. It too is rejected as default art. Functional correctness does not override the user's visual priority.

## Actual source-derived editable volumes

`export_cliff_volume_study.gd` exports three actual closed retained crowns near the front-left island edge, their world transforms, ordinary unified-base density nodes, local height/contour distances and a normal version4save. It does not invent replacement official stone geometry. Existing sources remain unchanged. Blender face subdivision permits a nonlinear inward lower return on copies of those solids; nearest-surface BVH distances with closed-solid parity are smoothly joined behind the original upper contour. Results are ordinary saved density samples loaded through `TerrainData.deserialize`, with canonical authored cells asserted unchanged. This is offline authoring of the same editable volume, not runtime models assembled after a player makes a mountain, and not a new player brush.

- v1:19,817samples. Cap-anchored1.55vertical extension with inward.60+.40×depth² return and a restrictive cap guard. It erases most shoulder shape, leaving a broad round wall, abrupt study-boundary notch and one detached small fragment. Rejected.
- v2:23,584samples. Quieter upper shoulder, then an inward.60+.10×depth+.55×max(depth−.8,0)² return; more permissive guard below the original top. Some shoulder projection survives, but wide faces still flow into an oversized round belly. Two detached fragments are visible in close/reverse/bottom. Rejected.
- v3:23,782samples. Same source geometry as v2, with two unequal open-top outward V fractures that taper into the lower body. Author-only negative-node component cleanup uses the actual tetrahedron edge graph and removes two isolated one-node components; changed-node magnitudes below.002 are moved away from lattice zero. No runtime mesher threshold changes. The visible loose fragments disappear, but the upper gaps read as artificial bites/steps with green triangular remnants and thin slot ridges. The bottom remains a dark broad swelling. Rejected despite the finite connectivity improvement.

## Consequence

More leaf coverage, wider cap masks, smooth unions or negative slots on these same stretched broad source proportions will not establish the target. The current source-to-volume route is not worth propagating. The next shape candidate must first show unequal broad planes with actual adjacent-face thickness, a shared ground boundary and an oblique lower return as a small solid seen from both sides. Do not spend another full-island capture sweep on minor parameter variations. There is no established external/user model-delivery blocker, and the goal remains active and artistically incomplete.
