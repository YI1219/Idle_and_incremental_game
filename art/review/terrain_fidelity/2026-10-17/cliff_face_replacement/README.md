## What changed the diagnosis

The eight front-envelope density studies replace a bounded side of the opt-in unified base. They use existing crown fronts, then the existing CC0 RockFace02 source, with rigid orientation/scale and lower return changes. All eight are rejected: shoulders, horizontal shelves, hanging plates, vertical pleats, round bellies or overly plain lower walls remain. The final source-cap variation still needs actual-skin grass coverage. More source placement or noise is not the next step.

The unified base itself differs substantially from the original model-based island. Patching its side had already lost the stronger original mass. A separate source export therefore records the original six body/shore/bed draw surfaces and 42 closed crown meshes, preserving all original bytes and cells. **It excludes high outcrop props, vegetation and buildings.** Unified mode also changes some decoration support/placement. None of these studies completely preserves the original scene silhouette or prop transforms.

Three-rays winding on that open/overlapping composite is unreliable: v1 disagrees on 14,591/126,665 classifications. A Blender .10m voxel-remesh has zero nonmanifold edges and consistent adjacent winding, yet classifications worsen to 30,388/125,583 disagreements. Independent float64 triangle intersections reproduce opposite-facing, coincident cap hits that iterative BVH stepping skips. The source is not a reliable solid union. The `closed` save is diagnostic failure evidence, **never rendered or accepted**. See `ray_diagnostic.json` in the [work directory](../../../../work/rendered_island/2026-10-17/).

The vertical-envelope replacement explicitly fills the entire interval between min/max analytic Y intersections at each .25m XZ node. That includes visible air under overhangs; it is **not shape-preserving or exact CSG**. `envelopecap` uses uniform vertical cap distance near the top so internal composite faces no longer create checker-like cap interpolation. The fixed cap study reduces 16 contour loops to two and removes most top checker patches. Native views recover more of the old unequal broad blocks than the derived round base, but retain a broad grey shoulder, soft panels, stacked lower support, right dangling bulk and a row of rim grass. Parent and independent review reject default promotion.

A separate `complete/` source then adds all 14 original high outcrop/shelf meshes (62 total components, 169,569 vertex occurrences), with the first 48 component bytes unchanged. The local `rendered_complete1` conversion restores those high rocks in the converted patch. This does not restore all other unified regions or decoration placement. It still has the same grey shoulder, soft surfaces and stacked lower mass; no promotion.

The single `retained_return1` trial replaces only lower-front density between actual complete-source front sections at Y1.5 and Y−3.5. Density at/above Y1.5 and at/below Y−3.5 is exactly unchanged; original cells, upper cap/root metadata and source high rocks are preserved. The inward power curve removes a left ledge, but fills a broad heavy wall/hull and leaves an endpoint hanging mass. Parent and independent review reject it. Do not perform further numeric curve sweeps. These later two versions have native-load and file/isolation checks only; earlier 12/12 edit fixtures and raw connectivity results must not be attributed to them.
