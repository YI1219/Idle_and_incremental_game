## Current visual implementation

The two terraces, rounded ponds, two falls, cottage and surface props follow the supplied composition. Broad cliff spans now subdivide within their stable endpoints at no more than .65 units. Narrow spans retain their existing profile. A world-anchored clipped-octahedron distance field produces stronger physical rock shoulders and recesses; the material uses matching site dimensions/metric and smaller oblique faces. Relief remains width-limited and cached per refresh. The first stronger-relief capture exposed a rock tongue through the lower waterfall. Wet walls and immediate mouth-side neighbors now limit protrusions behind the shared stream envelope, fading clearance at crests to preserve cap joins. Final captures remove that tongue. Embedded CC0 stones retain original model facets and share the terrain palette.

Waterfalls retain physical folds, smooth chunk normals, translucent downward flow and separate opaque pond shading. Kenney CC0 parts now supply 17-part solid crowns with seven unequal overlapping core masses and ten smaller rim clusters. Source-bound normalization, local three-axis tilt and height-varied tint replace the flat repeated tiers. Original forks retain their existing runtime lift. A fresh actual-mesh ray-parity probe covers all four transformed wood extrema (X-min/X-max/Z-min/Y-max) with the crown; it does not prove every branch segment or view. Retained CC0 parts also supply grass, shrubs, hanging foliage, reeds, lilies, paths, fences and flattened varied shore stones. The refined cottage retains its original geometry and material-role-based wall/gable panels. Bench and lantern remain placeholders. Original models are unmodified. The sky uses a generated cloud-only bitmap with its [source record and prompt](../../../source/skies/2026-10-05/cloud_backdrop_v01.json), plus neutral cubemap lighting.

Remaining differences include deep cliff scallops, pointed lower projections and elongated patches; distinct modular crown lobes and a still-rounded overall crown outline, repeated shore stones and simple lawns; rectangular falls and repeated splash forms; simpler cottage timber/eaves; and more detailed clouds than the scene props. Independent read-only review found no Critical/Important issue and judged the irregular silhouette closer to the reference. Inspected captures have no visibly detached clusters. The large left tree hides more branching and the small crowns remain nearly spherical. Review inspected source/captures/probe without independently rerunning the suite or benchmark. Human visual acceptance remains pending.
