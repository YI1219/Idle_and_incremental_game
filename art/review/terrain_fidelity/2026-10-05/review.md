## Current visual implementation

The two terraces, rounded ponds, two falls, cottage and surface props follow the supplied composition. Broad cliff spans now subdivide within their stable endpoints at no more than .65 units. Narrow spans retain their existing profile. A world-anchored clipped-octahedron distance field produces stronger physical rock shoulders and recesses; the material uses matching site dimensions/metric and smaller oblique faces. Relief remains width-limited and cached per refresh. The first stronger-relief capture exposed a rock tongue through the lower waterfall. Wet walls and immediate mouth-side neighbors now limit protrusions behind the shared stream envelope, fading clearance at crests to preserve cap joins. Final captures remove that tongue. Embedded CC0 stones retain original model facets and share the terrain palette.

Waterfalls retain physical folds, smooth chunk normals, translucent downward flow and separate opaque pond shading. Kenney CC0 parts now supply 17-part solid crowns with seven unequal overlapping core masses and ten smaller rim clusters. Source-bound normalization, local three-axis tilt and height-varied tint replace the flat repeated tiers. Original forks retain their existing runtime lift. The actual-mesh ray-parity probe at `800e55c` covered all four transformed wood extrema (X-min/X-max/Z-min/Y-max) with the crown; it does not prove every branch segment or view. Retained CC0 parts also supply grass, shrubs, hanging foliage, reeds, lilies, paths, fences and flattened varied shore stones. The refined cottage retains its original geometry and material-role-based wall/gable panels. Bench and lantern remain placeholders. Original models are unmodified. The sky retains the generated cloud-only bitmap with its [source record and prompt](../../../source/skies/2026-10-05/cloud_backdrop_v01.json). Runtime mipmaps and five LOD 2.5 taps soften fine billows; lower contrast and paler cyan fit the scene more closely. The first LOD 3.5 trial blurred too much and was reduced before final capture. Original PNGs/import metadata remain byte-identical. Neutral cubemap lighting, sunlight and ambient settings remain unchanged. Six sampled opaque roof/grass/crown/cliff/fence/wall pixels match the preceding full capture exactly; this is finite evidence, not whole-image identity.

Remaining differences include deep cliff scallops, pointed lower projections and elongated patches; distinct modular crown lobes and a still-rounded overall crown outline, repeated shore stones and simple lawns; rectangular falls and repeated splash forms; simpler cottage timber/eaves; and softer but still image-based clouds. The prior canopy review noted hidden branching and nearly spherical small crowns. Fresh independent read-only sky review found no Critical/Important issue, confirmed resource lifetime/fallback/lighting separation and judged the softer cyan background closer to the reference. Lower-left clouds are somewhat washed and the upper sky is flatter than the reference gradient. Review inspected source/captures without independently running tests/benchmark or judging alternate resolutions/startup cost. Human visual acceptance remains pending.
