# Floating-island terrain revision

Status: functional review passed; visual acceptance pending. The goal remains active.

The active workshop now uses shared rounded contours over sparse 2D height data, rather than a 3D voxel volume. Fixed vertical cliff samples and local profiles preserve distant rocks after local edits. Exposed grass caps subtract higher regions, including holes. An immutable revision builds two chunks per frame, then publishes its meshes, instanced detail, support props and picking together. Newer input coalesces into the next snapshot, so sustained painting continues to publish terrain.

These captures drain ordinary incremental updates and do not force a full rebuild to mask stale geometry. Each edit starts from the composition layout. [Capture metadata](captures.json) records brush centers, radii and changed cells. PNGs remain local per repository policy; the capture script reproduces them.

The wider upper terrace, shifted ponds and falls, supported trees, cottage, fences, bench and lantern now follow the reference composition more closely. Existing CC0 Kenney source models and the refined cottage remain unchanged. The supporting cliff body now carries stronger world-anchored material facets and rounded cellular shoulders, with occasional embedded stone_largeA models rather than repeated vertical stacks. The geometry/material site field uses matching 2.2×1.8×2.2 dimensions; relief caches by exact warped position within each refresh. Extra shoulders fade out on narrow profiles, retaining the existing one-cell-strip shape. Broad profiles have reduced directional and vertical wave noise. The latest actual cliff close-up breaks up long diagonal folds into more distinct rock regions, though broad smooth bulges and contrasting patches remain a modest improvement rather than a reproduction. Runtime canopies use 14 overlapping lobes above a taller, broader detailed-tree trunk, with a reduced ellipsoid blend (.48) and per-lobe tint. Existing forks are more visible. The retained low forks receive a runtime upward lift, with XZ coordinates, base vertices at Y≤.18 and topology preserved. This meets the raised crown without the extra low leaf clusters that hid the forks. A temporary actual-mesh ray-parity probe initially found two tips outside leaves; final fork lifting covers four sampled fork/stem endpoints. This checks the chosen points, not every branch segment or all view angles. Original GLB source files remain intact. Hanging foliage assembles flattened leaf sprays; shoreline stones use flatter stone_largeD parts with deterministic size and angle variation. Shorter grass and subtle lawn color facets calm the ground. The cottage has broader workshop proportions and warmer timber panels across both walls and gables. Its retained corrected-material names now separate plaster, timber and foundation stone from roof treatment, replacing the height-only/brightness classification. Object-space normals keep panel posts fixed when the view changes. The textured roof retains its source texture with a warmer runtime tint; no cottage geometry changed. Bench and lantern remain placeholders. No custom model-generation service or purchase was used. The built-in image-generation tool produced a cloud-only sky backdrop; its [source record and exact prompt](../../../source/skies/2026-10-05/cloud_backdrop_v01.json) accompany the runtime copy in `assets/terrain_editor/cloud_backdrop_v01.png`. The backdrop fills the fixed camera view and uses neutral cubemap lighting; terrain and props remain interactive 3D geometry.

Pond shallow-bank and floor-depth color derive from the same terrain snapshot. Its texture publishes alongside the completed mesh, keeping partial rebuilds from changing visible water early. This adds a shoreline appearance transition, not refraction or fluid simulation. Falling water uses finer moving polygonal veins (5.2×2.6 UV frequency) with varied line brightness, a lighter blue palette and reduced emission. The distortion depends entirely on advected flow coordinates, so the full pattern moves downward. Pixel-footprint smoothing replaces subpixel dotted highlights at distant views; the joined curtain geometry is unchanged. Independent review cleared the advection direction and shader correctness, but flagged excessive net brightness; the final glint strength was lowered from .86 to .55 and actual captures regenerated. The pattern remains visible and sheet-like; still images cannot establish temporal shimmer quality. Joined raised foam rings have interpolated smooth normals, a transparent edge and slight surface motion; chunk clipping preserves their opacity and normals. Independent review found no important remaining issue after the downward animation correction.

Visual differences remain: broad cliff areas are smoother than the target's broken rock volumes; tree crowns still show assembled rounded lobes, open lawns remain simpler, and shoreline stones repeat; falls still read as sheets with patterned veins and a modest splash; the cottage lacks finer timber bays and ornament; the detailed cloud backdrop contrasts with the simpler tree and cliff surfaces. This is a closer editable reconstruction, not an accepted reproduction. Fine custom-model changes remain subject to the user-operated asset workflow.

Fresh checks for this cottage refinement: the Metal render regression passes 7/7, runtime support checks 22/22 and camera constraints 8/8. Independent read-only review reran 7/7, inspected before/after cottage images and found no Important issue. It notes that layered timber, windows and eaves still differ from the target. Checksums confirm eight unrelated local files, eleven Kenney GLBs, the refined cottage and both received cottage originals remain unchanged. The eleven-script terrain suite was freshly verified at preceding revision `2535c6e`; it was not rerun for this material-only pass. The unchanged 28-script gameplay suite is older stage evidence. Measured on Apple M4 Pro, Metal Forward+, 1280×720, shadows and SSAO enabled, vsync disabled: three 12-second runs after two-second warmups. Median frame time 5.433–6.488 ms; p95 15.458–15.899 ms; worst sampled frame 77.427 ms. Four edits per run became visible in 119.081–151.499 ms. Every frame is sampled independently through normal two-chunk update waits. [Measurements](performance.json). Default terrain has 595 cells; samples end at 593 after edits. Draw calls include shadow passes (496). Static memory was about 57.9 MiB; reported video memory was 206.9 MiB, including the cloud texture. Capture runs use fixed-step timing and screenshot UI counters are not benchmark evidence. The preceding branch/crown pass measured 113.556–171.387 ms visible edits; run-to-run variation prevents a performance improvement claim from this cottage-material change.

These short development-machine samples do not establish large-layout scaling, target laptop performance, or long-idle power/stability. Function, visual quality and performance acceptance remain separate.
