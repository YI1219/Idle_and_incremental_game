Method: built-in image_gen. Each image was generated independently, not cropped from a collage. The front view is the identity reference. The first side drafts were too thick; left and right views were regenerated.

## Front

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: SINGLE FRONT ORTHOGRAPHIC REFERENCE IMAGE for multi-view Tripo 3D generation of the starter sword in a Chinese cultivation game.
Generate ONE object only: an elegant simple straight double-edged Chinese jian sword, bare blade, no scabbard, no character. Art direction: abstract beautiful large coherent matte color masses, restrained ancient Chinese design, low-poly-friendly silhouette, not realistic ornate weaponry. Long straight slate-silver blue-gray blade, a subtle broad central ridge represented by just two large tonal planes, tapered shoulders and one clean pointed tip. Compact simple muted jade green crossguard with gently curved ends (not giant wings), dark charcoal blue handle with a smooth surface, one small simple jade pommel. No tassels, ribbons, engravings, jewels, runes, leather-wrap detail, tiny ornaments, serrations, holes, cracks, bevel noise or shiny chrome.
Production framing: exact orthographic FRONT broad-face view, bilateral symmetry about vertical image center. Sword stands vertical, tip UP and pommel DOWN, broad flat blade face directly facing camera, no perspective or angle. Full object uncropped. Square canvas. Tip at y=10% canvas height, pommel bottom at y=90%, center x=50%. Overall object height 80% of canvas. Blade accounts for approximately 74% of object length, handle plus pommel 22%, guard zone 4%. Guard total span about 16% of object length; blade widest part about 6%. This is a one-handed light sword, graceful practical proportions.
Neutral uniform near-white background, shadowless soft studio light, no ground plane, cast shadow, glow, motion effects, text, labels, borders, scale ruler, UI or collage. Render as clean stylized 3D asset reference with clear edges and simple solid geometry. Output exactly ONE single-view image, not a turnaround sheet.
