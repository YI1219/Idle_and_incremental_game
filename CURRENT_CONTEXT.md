## Current workshop revision

Work is on `codex/terrain-fidelity` in the primary checkout. The old cell renderer remains as source/test evidence; the active renderer now uses shared smoothed contours at each elevation, with grass caps, tapered faceted cliff volumes, joined pond surfaces and adaptive spill curtains. Sampling is anchored to world positions; local-thickness taper avoids origin-dependent displacement and narrow-shell inversion. All geometry-owning chunks regenerate from an immutable snapshot, with two built each frame; terrain, foliage, supported props and picking publish together. Continuous painting coalesces newer edits while allowing the current snapshot to finish. Signed cap decomposition preserves small interior contractions and removes caps under higher ground. Fixed vertical cliff samples and local profiles keep distant rock shapes stable after small height edits. Picking intersects the rendered mesh and resolves protruding cliff hits to editable support cells.

The authoritative data remains a sparse 2D XZ height grid, not a 3D voxel volume. Raise/lower/expand/contract, optional snapping (off by default), eight water elevations, 47 neighbor data states, undo/redo and isolated layout save/load remain available. ## Confirmed combat principles (kept, not the current expansion focus)

Early game uses a held sword; later, sword flight may be considered. One-on-one fights can be handled calmly through observation and parry; multiple enemies are dangerous because direction and timing are hard to cover at once. Hostile characters' close-range spiritual pressure can stack and drain ward. Movement arts have charges and a short invulnerability window; advanced arts and realms may affect abilities.

Any positive ward before a hit grants full damage reduction determined by realm relation, envisioned as 60%–95%. The reduced portion is paid by ward, floored at zero; any reduction ward cannot cover is not transferred to vitality. If ward is zero on the next hit, there is no reduction. Active qi damage drains ward first and overflows to vitality; passive close-range spiritual pressure drains ward only. Exact numbers, recovery times, and skill parameters are tunable prototype values, not all locked. Details: [Combat rules and trial values](docs/combat.md).
